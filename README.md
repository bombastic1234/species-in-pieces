# Species in Pieces 🦎🦁🐝

Welcome to the "Species in Pieces" repository! This project showcases 30 species in 30 fragmented pieces to symbolize the challenges they face in survival. Each piece represents a unique aspect of these incredible creatures, highlighting the beauty and diversity of the animal kingdom.

## Repository Contents 🌿

In this repository, you will find the following:

- Stunning digital representations of 30 different species.
- Fragmented pieces that come together to form a captivating visual narrative.
- A deep dive into the world of each species and the threats they encounter.

## Repository Overview 🐢

"Species in Pieces" aims to raise awareness about the importance of biodiversity and the need to protect our planet's wildlife. Through art and storytelling, we hope to inspire action and spark conversations about conservation efforts.
